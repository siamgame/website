Educational Mini-Games

Have fun while learning with these exciting educational games from around the web!

  • PBS Kids Math Games - A variety of games focusing on numbers, shapes, and problem-solving.
  • National Geographic Kids Quizzes - Test your knowledge on animals, science, and geography.
  • NASA STEM for Students (K-4) - Engage with space and science through games and activities from NASA.
  • Highlights Kids Games - Puzzles, matching games, and hidden pictures that are fun and educational.
  • Funbrain Games - Math and reading games for various age levels, including classics like Math Baseball.
  • ABCya! - A wide range of educational games for PreK to 6th grade, covering letters, numbers, and more.
  • Toy Theater - Collection of educational games for elementary school students focusing on art, math, and reading.
  • Starfall - Primarily focused on reading, with activities for phonics, word recognition, and comprehension.
  • Coolmath Games - Logic, skill, and number games designed to make math fun. (Note: Some games may be less strictly educational).
  • Sesame Street Games - Educational games featuring beloved Sesame Street characters, focusing on basic skills for preschoolers.
  • PrimaryGames - A variety of learning games for elementary and middle school students covering math, reading, science, and social studies.
